In view

    Facilities: CMS Provider of Services files (Medicare-certified hospitals and surgery centers, active as of the July 2026 quarter) and CMS Care Compare for VA and military hospitals.

    Projects: Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) TABS registry, searched Oct 22, 2026. Texas requires every building project of $50,000 or more to be registered there, with its owner, design firm, cost and estimated start and finish dates, before construction. Stages come from those estimated dates, so a project can run behind its filing. “Announced” projects come from news reports and have no state filing yet.
